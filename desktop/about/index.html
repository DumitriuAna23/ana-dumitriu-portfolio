HOW I THINK

I start with what actually needs fixing.

When I enter an existing project, my first instinct is not to rewrite it. I test it in different situations, look for the places where the product really fails and try to understand the logic behind the current decisions.

I use AI as a second perspective, not as an automatic answer. Before applying a suggestion, I compare it with the architecture, the coding style and the real behavior of the product.

And when something is unclear or undocumented, I like turning it into a structure I can reason about — notes, comments, flows and small systems — before changing anything important.
