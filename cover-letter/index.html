Dear Hiring Team,

I’m Ana-Maria Dumitriu, a third-year engineering student and software developer with hands-on experience building full-stack applications, AI-assisted product flows, embedded systems and connected hardware. I’m currently looking for a fully remote role where I can contribute as a developer and continue growing through real product work.

I intentionally keep the title of that role open. I’m interested in frontend, backend, full-stack, Python, TypeScript / JavaScript, applied AI, embedded software and adjacent technical positions where my current skill set can be useful. What matters more to me than a narrow job label is the opportunity to understand a product, solve real problems and take responsibility for the quality of what I build.

My current work reflects that range. I have built and deployed AutoDiagnose AI, a full-stack diagnostic platform involving responsive interfaces, REST APIs, authentication, session management, relational data and diagnostic reasoning. I also developed ELIO, a connected educational robot that combines ESP32 firmware, sensors, motor control, BLE communication, physical prototyping and companion software. Those projects taught me to think beyond one layer of a system and to follow problems across frontend, backend, data, hardware and deployment when necessary.

HOW I WORK

I prefer to understand before I rewrite. I test what already exists, identify the real failure first and only then decide what should change.

When I join an existing codebase, I start by testing the product in different situations and looking for issues that actually affect the user. I try to understand the architecture, the conventions already in place and the reasoning behind the current implementation. If an area is unclear or undocumented, I structure it before making large changes. I use AI as a second perspective and a productivity tool, but not as a replacement for technical judgment: suggestions still need to fit the system, the codebase and the real behavior of the product.

Because I’m currently in the third year of my degree, I’m particularly interested in fully remote roles that are evaluated primarily through ownership, communication, deadlines and results rather than continuous presence at a desk during every minute of a fixed schedule. I can organize my university responsibilities around planned meetings, collaboration windows and agreed deliverables. This flexibility would not reduce the volume, seriousness or quality of my work — I’m looking for a structure in which I can meet professional expectations consistently while continuing my degree.

Outside my personal software projects, I work as an Assistant Manager and Technical Trainer at Tales & Tech HUB, where I contribute to software and embedded projects and deliver hands-on workshops in programming, Arduino and robotics. That experience has strengthened my ability to communicate clearly, troubleshoot in real time and adapt technical concepts to the person or problem in front of me.

I’m looking for a team that values people who learn quickly, ask useful questions, care about the actual product and are willing to work across boundaries when the problem requires it. If that matches the way your team works, I’d be glad to discuss where I could contribute.
