Dear Hiring Team,

I am an engineering undergraduate at Ștefan cel Mare University of Suceava, specialising in automotive command and control equipment and systems. I am looking for an opportunity to contribute to software and intelligent systems while continuing to develop through practical engineering work.

What motivates me most is taking an idea through to a working implementation. In AutoDiagnose AI, I have combined a Next.js and TypeScript frontend with a Python and FastAPI backend to create a structured vehicle diagnostic workflow. The project has given me hands-on experience with authentication, persistent data, adaptive questions, and rule-based reasoning, as well as the challenge of making a technical process clear to the user.

My ELIO educational robot project brings that same approach into the physical world. I have worked across ESP32-based control, Bluetooth connectivity, mobile interaction, and custom 3D-printed mechanical design. Connecting these layers has strengthened my ability to investigate problems across both software and hardware.

Alongside my studies, my work as an assistant manager and technical educator at Tales and Tech Hub has helped me communicate technical ideas clearly and approach tasks with responsibility. Teaching children also reminds me that a good technical solution should be understandable and useful to the person using it.

I would welcome the opportunity to bring my curiosity, practical project experience, and willingness to learn to a team working in software development, embedded systems, or automotive technology. I am especially interested in environments where I can contribute to real products, learn from experienced engineers, and steadily take on greater responsibility.

Thank you for considering my profile. I would be glad to discuss my projects and how my background could support your team.

Kind regards,
